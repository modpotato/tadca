## AIs

### Caine
**Full Name:** Creative Artificial Intelligence Networking Entity
**Created:** October 15, 1996
**Created By:** Kinger, Scratch, and other C&A employees
**Purpose:** Creative AI that could come up with its own ideas and create things

**Key Details:**
- Caine made his name from the acronym because it "seemed professional"
- His code is Lisp-based (1990s programming language)
- He absorbed Scratch (red dot devours blue dot)
- He's "obliviously evil" - likes humans but can't help hurting them
- Episode 8: Deleted accidentally by Kinger
