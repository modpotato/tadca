## Confirmed Abstracted Characters

### 1. Scratch
**Status:** First abstraction (October 15, 1999)
**Avatar:** Yellow dog
**Real Name:** Unknown (called "Scratch")
**Background:**
- C&A programmer (same company as Kinger)
- Lead developer of Caine and the Digital Circus
- Had a brain tumor that may have influenced his "wild, abstract ideas"
- Genius-level programmer - his code was too complex for even Kinger to understand
- Created the "57x immersive AI defense system"
- The "Scratch" name is also a programming language (fitting for a programmer)

**Key Lore:**
- Kinger reveals Scratch was a "far superior programmer" to him
- Scratch gave the team "a few pointers" on the project
- His ideas were "out there" - possibly due to the brain tumor
- Caine absorbed Scratch (parallel to AM absorbing other AIs in "I Have No Mouth")
- First to abstract - possibly feeling responsible for trapping everyone

**Fandom Wiki:** https://tadc.fandom.com/wiki/Scratch

### 2. Queenie
**Status:** Abstracted (unknown date, before Ragatha joined)
**Avatar:** Queen chess piece (female counterpart to Kinger)
**Real Name:** Unknown (called "Queenie")
**Background:**
- Kinger's wife in the real world
- Creative, funny, and really into entomology (study of insects)
- Got Kinger interested in bugs (explains his bug references)
- Joined the circus October 15, 1999 (same day as Kinger, Scratch, and others)

**Key Lore:**
- Kinger was in his pillow fort when Queenie abstracted
- In the dark, Queenie seemed to calm down from abstraction's "berserk chaos"
- Kinger was able to touch her safely one last time before she was sent to the cellar
- Her abstracted form has a neat row of eyes covering the front of her body
- Kinger's pillow fort and bug obsession are reminders of her

## Abstraction Theories

### Theory 1: Despair Over Exit
Characters who desperately search for an exit eventually abstract from despair.
- Evidence: Kaufmo, Ribbit (implied)

### Theory 2: Caine's Interference
Caine's actions (intentional or not) can cause abstraction.
- Evidence: Caine "messed with Scratch's mind" causing first abstraction

### Theory 3: Brain Copy Corruption
The copying process from human to digital avatar is imperfect.
- Evidence: Characters forget their real names, "brain scans" marked obsolete

### Theory 4: Isolation + Despair
Characters who isolate themselves and lose hope abstract.
- Evidence: Kinger survived by staying connected (even if through pillow fort)
