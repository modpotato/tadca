### 3. Number 57

**Status:** CONFIRMED IN SHOW
**Significance:** Creator's favorite number, retroactively given lore meaning

**Locations Found:**
- Pilot: Jax's room
- Episode 4: $57.57 hot dog at Spudsy's, Orbsman orders "number 57"
- Episode 7: Abel's locket contains hot dog costing $57.57
- Website: Multiple $57.57 price tags
- Episode 8 Terminal: "57x immersive AI defense system"

**Connection to Monkey Wrench:**
- Number 57 appears in Gooseworx's earlier series "Monkey Wrench"
- Gooseworx confirmed it's her "favorite number/inside joke"

### 7. Chinese Room

**Location:** Episode 7 (Beach Episode)
**Reference:** John Searle's Chinese Room thought experiment (1980)

**Context:**
- Caine consults the "Chinese Room" for suggestions
- Door was unlocked the whole time
- Just a mannequin with Chinese dictionary
- Chinese characters translate to "egg", "help", "pain"

**Connection:** Questions whether AI can truly "understand" or just simulate understanding.

---

### 8. Daisy Bell (Bicycle Built for Two)

**Location:** Episode 6 (They All Get Guns)
**Context:** Pomni and Jax sing duet

**Reference:** 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968)
- HAL 9000 sings this as he's being deactivated
- Same theme of AI shutdown/deletion

### 9. Cain & Abel (Biblical Reference)

### 10. "I Have No Mouth"
