### Bubble

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Role | Caine's assistant (or parasite?) |
| Relationship | "The lesser of the two" |
| Created | 1996 (code), appeared post-2015 |

**Key Evidence:**
- Absent from early flashbacks (before Ragatha)
- Only appears when Pomni arrives
- Hacks Kinger's computer and knows his real name "Grant"
- Terminal username: 6-letter name ending in "e"
- Caine calls him a "parasite" in the pilot

**Episode 8:**
> Bubble tells Caine: "You really were the lesser of the two. You ruined this."

**Theory: Bubble = Scratch**
- Bubble may be Scratch's consciousness transferred into a vessel
- Scratch was "bizarre yet genius" — Bubble acts bizarre but hacks firewalls
- Timeline: Bubble's code created 1996, Scratch abstracted 1999, Bubble appears post-2015
- Caine may have experimented on Scratch's abstracted form, creating Bubble
- Bubble acts as parasite, waiting to dismantle the circus from within

**Two AIs Theory:**
- Blue AI = Scratch's creation (the stable, intended ringmaster)
- Caine = inferior AI that consumed the Blue AI
- Caine ruined the intended program
- Bubble (Scratch) is plotting revenge
