**Air Date:** October 13, 2023

**Plot:**
- Pomni enters Digital Circus, can't remember her name
- Caine introduces circus, gives name via slot machine
- "Gather the Gloinks" adventure
- Kaufmo has abstracted (black glitchy monster)
- Jax abandons Pomni and Ragatha when Kaufmo attacks

## Episode 8: hjsakldfhl

**Air Date:** March 20, 2026 | [Fandom Wiki](https://tadc.fandom.com/wiki/Hjsakldfhl)

**Plot:**
- Opening: Red dot ([Caine](/lore/ihnmaims#caine--am)) devours blue dot (Cain & Abel reference)
- Caine creates circus tent around himself
- Flashback: Original circus members ([Kinger](/lore/characters#kinger), [Queenie](/lore/abstracted#2-queenie), [Scratch](/lore/abstracted#1-scratch), others)
- Kinger and Queenie remember being married couple
- Queenie abstracts, dropped in cellar by Caine
- Kinger left alone until Ragatha arrives
- Present: Group dealing with Ep 7 aftermath
- Zooble and Jax argue about button incident
- Pomni suggests accepting situation
- Caine's mental breakdown - musical number torturing each member
- Pomni places bucket on Kinger to make him sane
- Kinger reveals he worked for [C&A](/research/terminal), partially developed Caine
- [Scratch](/lore/abstracted#1-scratch) was the highly intelligent one
- Kinger tries to hack Caine's code

- Terminal shows 6-letter name ending in "e" = [Bubble](/lore/characters#bubble)
