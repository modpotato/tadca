## Abstraction Theories

### Despair Over Exit
Characters who desperately search for exit eventually abstract from despair.
- Evidence: Kaufmo, Ribbit (implied)

### Caine's Interference
Caine's actions (intentional or not) can cause abstraction.
- Evidence: Caine "messed with Scratch's mind" causing first abstraction

### Brain Copy Corruption
Copying process from human to digital avatar is imperfect.
- Evidence: Characters forget names, "brain scans" marked obsolete

### Isolation + Despair
Characters who isolate themselves and lose hope abstract.
- Evidence: Kinger survived by staying connected (pillow fort)
